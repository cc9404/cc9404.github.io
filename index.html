Feature 04 · Marketing analytics

Market Basket Analysis

Goal: find which products customers buy together, and use those pairings to design bundles and cross-sell offers.

  • Apriori association rules on 380 French transaction baskets covering 1,541 products.
  • Filtered for lift of at least 2.0 and confidence of at least 60%.
  • Recommended partyware bundles, tiered collector pricing, and checkout cross-sells.

The network graph separates the catalog into distinct communities, such as partyware, alarm clocks, and children's cutlery. The lift heatmap shows strong co-purchase within each community, which points to bundles inside the group and cross-sells between them.
